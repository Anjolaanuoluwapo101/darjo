# D'arjo — Product Walkthrough & Demo Video Script

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds  
**Target Viewport:** Mobile Emulation (iPhone 12/14 — 390px width)

---

## 🎬 Scene Breakdown & Recording Checklist

### Scene 1: Introduction & Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual:** D'arjo landing view / Profile Screen (`http://localhost:3000/profile`).
- **Audio / Script:** 
  > "Traditional rotating savings groups, or ROSCAs, power financial inclusion for millions in emerging markets. But they lack credit visibility and suffer from currency instability. D'arjo bridges this gap by bringing automated credit scoring and currency volatility protections into cross-border ROSCAs."

---

### Scene 2: Profile & Credit Score Ring (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Visual:** Focus on the animated `ScoreRing` component and Tier progression card on `/profile`.
- **Audio / Script:**
  > "Here on the Profile screen, users see their live D'arjo Score—a dynamic rating built on past on-time ROSCA contributions. As users complete payout cycles without delay, their score advances through Silver and Gold tiers, unlocking higher borrowing limits across circles."

---

### Scene 3: AI Assistant & Intent Nudges (1:00 - 1:45)
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/assistant`. Click the quick chip *"Draft a friendly payment nudge"*, then tap *"Show active circle details"*.
- **Audio / Script:**
  > "Our integrated Gemini-powered AI Assistant acts as a personal financial co-pilot. Members can ask for instant breakdowns of their credit score growth, draft automated payment reminders for peers, or inspect active circle health directly within the chat."

---

### Scene 4: Slide-Up Circle Drawer & Volatility Safeguards (1:45 - 2:15)
- **Visual:** Tap **Inspect Circle Drawer** on the chat message card to trigger the slide-up modal (`CircleDrawer`). Point to the USD/NGN volatility rating and PayPal payment disclaimer.
- **Audio / Script:**
  > "Tapping into the Circle Drawer reveals live circle parameters, contribution schedules, and real-time exchange rate volatility flags—ensuring cross-border members are protected against unexpected currency drops before payouts trigger."

---

### Scene 5: Outro & Call to Action (2:15 - 2:30)
- **Visual:** Return to main profile overview showing completed transaction history log.
- **Audio / Script:**
  > "D'arjo makes community saving accountable, automated, and cross-border ready. Thanks for watching!"
